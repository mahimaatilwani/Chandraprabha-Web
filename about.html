About Us

Crafting Meaningful Stays in Rajasthan.

THE BEGINNING

Our Story

Chandraprabha Resorts & Hotels was established with a singular vision: to craft boutique sanctuaries that offer travelers a profound connection to Rajasthan’s raw, untamed beauty. Born out of a deep-rooted love for the dramatic granite monoliths and quiet wilderness of Jawai, our stays are designed to merge seamlessly into the landscape rather than dominate it.

CULTURAL HERITAGE

Inspired by Rajasthan

Our hospitality is inspired by the ancient Rajasthani belief of 'Atithi Devo Bhava'—treating every guest as a divine visitor. We invite travelers to slow down and immerse themselves in the living folklore, dynamic festivals, and timeless stories carved in the sandstone landscapes of Rajasthan.

  • Heritage
  • Culture
  • Hospitality
CONSERVATION PHILOSOPHY

Designed Around Nature

We believe true luxury lies in absolute harmony with the wilderness. Our boutique glamping tents and stone cottages are built using local materials and run with low-impact footprints, ensuring that the natural migration corridors and habitats of Jawai's wildlife remain untouched and respected.

  • Wildlife
  • Conservation
  • Sustainable Luxury
LEADERSHIP

Meet the Founder

Mr. Chandraveer was inspired by a lifelong fascination with the delicate ecosystem of Jawai Bandh, where wild leopards, migratory birds, and nomadic Rabari shepherds have coexisted peacefully for generations. We borrow this earth from our children, and our commitment is to leave it richer, wilder, and more beautiful.

Mr. Chandraveer Singh Mertiya Founder of Chandraprabha Group
