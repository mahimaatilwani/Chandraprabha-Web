About Us

A Wilderness Escape

in Jawai Hills

Welcome to Chandraprabha Resort & Hotel, where the timeless beauty of Jawai meets refined hospitality. Surrounded by majestic granite hills, thriving wildlife, and serene landscapes, our resort offers an escape into nature without compromising on comfort.

Whether you're seeking the thrill of a leopard safari, peaceful moments under open skies, or authentic Rajasthani hospitality, every stay is thoughtfully designed to reconnect you with the wilderness while indulging you in modern luxury.
