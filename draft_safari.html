The Jawai Experience

A rare sanctuary where ancient stone, historic tribes, and silent predators exist in absolute, peaceful harmony.

Jawai Bandh in Rajasthan represents a stunning anomaly in wildlife conservation. Unlike any other safari park, leopards here thrive not within dense forests, but on exposed, prehistoric granite hills. Living in close quarters with the traditional Rabari nomadic shepherds, these majestic big cats have fostered an ancient, sacred bond of peaceful coexistence, making Jawai one of the most unique leopard-spotting destinations on earth.

Ecosystem Residents

Wildlife of Jawai

Beyond the leopard, Jawai shelter a fascinating assortment of dry-scrubland predators, raptors, and marshland reptiles.

Found in Jawai

Indian Leopard

Adapted to living entirely on sheer granite caves and rock walls rather than trees.

Found in Jawai

Striped Hyena

Elusive nocturnal hunters that inhabit caves inside isolated rock clusters.

Found in Jawai

Marsh Crocodile

Over 100 giant muggers share the waters and shores of the Jawai Bandh Reservoir.
