Five Legendary Properties

Luxury Safari — Jawai

Chandraprabha Resort

"Where the untamed wild meets royal glamping luxury."

Nestled in the granite valleys of Jawai, Chandraprabha Resort is our flagship wilderness sanctuary. Experience high-end eco-glamping in custom-designed tents that offer stunning views of the surrounding hills, placing you at the heart of leopard territory.

  • Private luxury glamping suites
  • Bespoke open-top jeep safaris
  • Rabari tribal village guided tours
  • Bush dining under the desert sky

Mountain Escape — Jawai

Sky Jawai Resort

"Elevated serenity on the granite monoliths of Jawai."

Perched high upon the ancient rock clusters, Sky Jawai Resort offers sweeping panoramic views of Jawai Bandh and the surrounding plains. Designed for peace and elevation, it serves as the ultimate high-altitude wilderness retreat.

  • Panoramic private viewing decks
  • Granite peak high-tea setups
  • Premium hill-view cottages
  • High-altitude infinity pool

Nature Escape — Kumbhalgarh

Kumbhal Van Resort

"Forest tranquility nestled near historical fort walls."

Located in the cool Aravalli hills near the grand Kumbhalgarh Fort, Kumbhal Van Resort is a lush sanctuary. The property features verdant private gardens, forest trails, and cool mountain air, offering an intimate communion with nature.

  • Valley-facing luxury chalets
  • Fort wall historical guided walks
  • Organic farm-to-table dining
  • Birdwatching & forest treks

Royal Heritage — Jaipur

Signature Resort

"Immerse yourself in legendary Rajputana grandeur."

Signature Resort in Jaipur is a majestic heritage-style retreat. Combining traditional architecture, expansive event lawns, and premium wellness spas, it captures the royal hospitality and color of Rajasthan's capital.

  • Sprawling palace-style courtyard
  • Royal wellness spa treatments
  • Temperature-controlled pools
  • Sprawling heritage event lawns

City Comfort — Udaipur

Hotel Meghdoot

"An elegant urban oasis in the City of Lakes."

Perfectly located in Udaipur, Hotel Meghdoot blends local heritage aesthetics with modern convenience. It serves as a peaceful urban gateway for exploring the historic streets, temples, and palace lakes of Udaipur.

  • Lake-view rooftop dining deck
  • Traditional courtyard architecture
  • Located near major palace sights
  • Modern luxury suites & amenities
